    Accessible location list

    ⓘ Search currently covers mapped places within approximately 5 km of central Almaty, not the whole city. Up to 350 entries are retrieved per request. Places are fetched from community-maintained OpenStreetMap data via the Overpass API. Entries, addresses, opening hours and accessibility features may be incomplete or outdated. Map markers do not guarantee step-free or safe pedestrian access. Verify details before travelling. Location access is optional.

    ACCESSIBILITY INFORMATION

    Understand before you go

    Each place has an Accessibility Details panel with OpenStreetMap tags for wheelchair access, tactile paving, and other useful features. Missing data is marked Not specified — never assumed accessible.

    ✓

    Reported accessible

    A community map entry explicitly indicates a feature is available. It has not been independently checked by City Eyes.

    !

    Reported not accessible

    A community map entry explicitly indicates that a feature is unavailable.

    ?

    Not specified

    No relevant accessibility tag is available. Do not interpret missing data as safe or accessible.

    For navigation, use official transport and facility information where available. City Eyes cannot guarantee safe routes, accessible entrances, or functioning audible signals.
